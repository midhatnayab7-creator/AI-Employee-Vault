# Rainfall & Precipitation Report: 2025 — 2026
### Prepared by Atlas | 2026-02-16 | Midhat's Group

---

## Overview

2025 was a **La Nina year** with extreme rainfall contrasts — record floods in some regions, expanding drought in others. Global precipitation was near the long-term mean, but individual events were historically devastating.

- **4,000+ flash flood warnings** issued by NWS by end of July 2025 (most since records began in 1986)
- **1,434 flash flood warnings** in July alone (2nd highest July total in 40 years)
- Multiple **1-in-1,000-year rainfall events**
- **135+ deaths** from Texas Hill Country flooding alone

## Monthly Rainfall Breakdown by Season

### Winter 2024-2025 (Dec — Feb)

| Month | Rainfall Pattern | Key Events |
|-------|-----------------|------------|
| Dec 2024 | Above average (West Coast) | Atmospheric rivers hitting California |
| Jan 2025 | Below average (SoCal), dry winds | LA wildfires fueled by Santa Ana winds, no rain |
| Feb 2025 | Above average (West), strong ARs | Atmospheric rivers — Mad River, Eel River, Russian River exceeded flood stages |

**Rain Days (U.S. Average):** ~8-12 days/month
**Notable:** February atmospheric rivers brought widespread flooding to Northern California. Rivers at Arcata, Fernbridge, Hopland, and Tehama Bridge all exceeded flood stages.

---

### Spring 2025 (Mar — May)

| Month | Rainfall Pattern | Key Events |
|-------|-----------------|------------|
| Mar 2025 | Dry (Central U.S.), extreme winds | Drought + wildfires in TX/OK — humidity as low as 10% |
| Apr 2025 | Extreme rainfall (South/Midwest) | Apr 2-7: Historic flooding + tornado outbreak |
| May 2025 | Above average (South Asia monsoon early) | Early South Asian monsoon, heavy rain in N. South America |

**Rain Days (U.S. Average):** ~9-14 days/month
**Notable:** April's stalled fronts produced historic rainfall across the Lower Ohio Valley. Parts of Kentucky and Tennessee saw 1-in-1,000-year rainfall.

---

### Summer 2025 (Jun — Aug)

| Month | Rainfall Pattern | Key Events |
|-------|-----------------|------------|
| Jun 2025 | Extreme localized storms | Severe storm season ramped up — tornadoes + rain |
| Jul 2025 | Record flash flooding | 1,434 flash flood warnings (2nd highest July ever) |
| Aug 2025 | Continued flooding + heat | Texas Hill Country: ~2 feet of rain in days → 135+ deaths |

**Rain Days (U.S. Average):** ~8-12 days/month
**Notable:** NWS issued almost 4,000 flash flood warnings by end of July — more than any year since comparable records began in 1986. The Texas Hill Country experienced a 1-in-100 to 1-in-1,000-year flood event.

---

### Fall 2025 (Sep — Nov)

| Month | Rainfall Pattern | Key Events |
|-------|-----------------|------------|
| Sep 2025 | Hurricane-driven rain | Hurricane season intensified — Cat 4-5 storms |
| Oct 2025 | Extreme tropical rainfall | Hurricane Melissa (Cat 5) — massive rainfall in Caribbean |
| Nov 2025 | Above average (South Asia/Pacific) | Large positive anomalies across South Asia and western Pacific |

**Rain Days (U.S. Average):** ~7-11 days/month
**Notable:** Cyclone Senyar brought catastrophic rainfall to Thailand and Indonesia (1,500+ deaths). North Carolina saw 1-in-1,000-year rainfall recurrence.

---

### Winter 2025-2026 (Dec — Feb)

| Month | Rainfall Pattern | Key Events |
|-------|-----------------|------------|
| Dec 2025 | At global mean, rain instead of snow | Warm temps caused rain instead of snow — snow drought in mountains |
| Jan 2026 | Above average (storm belt) | Jan 24-26 mega winter storm — 20+ states, heavy precip |
| Feb 2026 | Mixed — drought expanding in Plains | Central/High Plains drought continuing; wet East Coast |

**Rain/Snow Days (U.S. Average):** ~8-13 days/month
**Notable:** Despite wetter-than-normal conditions in the West, warmer temperatures meant rain fell instead of snow, leading to snow drought in many mountain basins.
